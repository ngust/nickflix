# Changelog

All dates 2026. NickFlix is a Netflix-style front end for an Xtream-codes IPTV account on Samsung Tizen TVs.

## 4.11
- **Settings redesign.** A category list on the left (Profile, Content & filters, Live TV, Picks & Surprise me, Sports & time, Playback, App & intro, Account) and a detail pane on the right. Big focus ring, the same switch style for every On/Off setting, a one-line description under each row, a hint bar ("◀ or BACK returns to the list"), and focus is remembered per category (and the last category is reopened). ◀ from the first column or BACK goes back one level; BACK on the list closes Settings. Every 4.10 setting is still there; "Reset learning" moved from Sports to Profile (press twice to confirm).
- **Profile screens polished.** Change avatar / Edit name straight from Settings › Profile and return there; the profile editor, Who's watching and dialogs got rounder buttons and clearer focus.
- **235 avatars in 20 groups** (108 new, all original art generated by `tools/mkav3.py`: wild animals, ocean, birds & bugs, dinosaurs, vehicles, more sports, music & games, nature & weather, fun characters, patterns & colours). The picker has a category rail (▲▼ category, ◀▶ browse) and a preview of the current pick. Existing avatar ids 0–126 are unchanged, so every profile keeps its avatar.
- **📅 Today's picks** row in Live TV, Movies and Shows (`js/picks.js`). It changes every day at midnight in the app's time zone (default Creston, UTC−7) and is seeded by date + profile, so it is stable all day. It is deliberately different from "Because you watched" and "Top picks": it uses the watch-history taste model but skips anything watched, anything in the top of Top picks, and anything shown in the last 3 days, then mixes "For your taste", "Try something new", "Favourite you've missed", "Fresh pick" and a few "Wild card" items (genre cap, reason cap, no duplicate posters). Settings › Picks: "Today's picks rows" (default On).
- **🎰 Surprise me** slot machine in Live TV (one press ▲ from the first row), Movies and Shows (next to Curated | All). Nothing is shown until you press it: three reels spin (CSS transforms only) with light generated clicks and dings, then land on taste-weighted random picks. OK on a pick plays the channel or opens the details page; Spin again; Close/BACK at any time. Settings › Picks: "Surprise me button" (default On; Off hides the button), "Surprise picks" (Taste / Balanced / Wild), "Slot machine sound".
- New per-profile keys only (`st.surprise`, `st.slotMix`, `st.slotSound`, `st.daily`, `dp`); nothing existing in localStorage is renamed or removed.
- `config.xml` version is now 4.11.0 (it had stayed at 2.0.0 since 2.0).
- `tests/t411.py` UI test for the above.

## 4.10.1
- Buffering in the first 12 s after playback starts or after any seek (resume, ◀ ▶ jumps, scrubbing) is not counted as a stall, so it can't trigger a refill or step-down. This was found on the TV: a resumed My Media title paused for a refill right after starting.

## 4.10
Built for a far-away provider (Europe, ~180 ms RTT, 1 connection) that drops out: trade a little live delay for stability, and optionally put a buffering proxy on the home NAS.
- **New default buffer: Extra-stable.** Live waits for 16 s of video before starting (so it plays about 15–20 s behind live) and refills 15 s after an underrun. Movies and shows wait for 20 s / 30 s. **Maximum** is now 30 s live / 40–45 s VOD. Normal and Large are kept. Anyone still on the old default (Large) or Normal moves to Extra-stable once; Maximum stays. The AVPlay buffering timeout (30 s / 45 s) and the prepare timeout now grow with the buffer, so a slow fill is no longer cut short and played on a half-full buffer.
- **Pause and refill instead of stutter:** a second stall within 2 minutes pauses playback on purpose for 10 s (Extra-stable; 15 s on Maximum, ×1.5 when it keeps happening) to build a bigger cushion, with a countdown badge. OK resumes right away. On HLS live this only happens when the provider's live window is 60 s or longer, so playback can't fall off the back of the playlist.
- **Automatic step-down** when trouble keeps coming (3 stalls in 5 min, 2 reconnects in 10 min, or 2 stalls plus 1 reconnect), at most once every 3 minutes, in this order: cap the HLS bitrate one level lower (when `AVAILABLE_BITRATE` lists several), then switch to a lower-quality copy of the same channel from the provider (FHD → HD → SD, using the already-loaded channel index), then the other format (HLS ↔ TS, Auto only, once), then a deeper buffer (×1.5 / ×1.75) for the session. Each step shows a toast and is logged. The bitrate cap is lifted one level after 15 minutes without stalls, at the next reconnect (no forced interruption).
- **Smoother reconnects:** after any trouble, HLS reopens with `STARTBITRATE=LOWEST` (fills fast, then climbs). There is a longer gap before reopening (2.5 s on Extra-stable, 3 s on Maximum) so the provider releases the single connection. VOD resumes 2 s before where it stopped.
- **Format learning:** watch time and stalls/drops per format are kept on the TV (`nf_fmtstat`, rolling ~20 h). Auto puts the clearly steadier format first (both formats need ≥ 45 min of data and less than half the trouble rate).
- **Home buffering proxy support:** `proxyUrls` in `config.js`, plus Settings › Playback › "Home buffering proxy" On/Off (default Off) and an address picker. When On, every live, movie and series stream goes to the NAS proxy (live is always requested as MPEG-TS). If the proxy doesn't answer `/health`, NickFlix plays direct and says so. Health checks are LAN-only and run only while nothing is playing. `tools/build.py` adds the proxy origins to `config.xml`.
- **`proxy/nfproxy.py`** (new, Python standard library only): a home buffering proxy that is the only client of the provider. Live streams go into a 96 MB RAM ring and the TV gets a 20 s burst behind live. Provider drops are reconnected with backoff while the TV plays from the ring. It switches the upstream between TS and HLS after 3 failures in a row. VOD uses read-ahead with exact-byte `Range` resume. A global single-upstream lock with a release gap and idle close keeps it to one provider connection. `/health` and `/status` endpoints are included, along with a Dockerfile, docker-compose (Container Station), a native start script and a fake-provider test (`proxy/test`).
- Settings › Playback copy rewritten. The 24 h summary now also counts buffer refills and quality step-downs.

## 4.9
- **Mini intro before shows** (`js/mini.js`): a 2.2 s sting in the 4.8 style (logo slams in from depth, a hit with flash and shockwave, a quick dive through light ribbons) with a short generated sound. It plays on top while AVPlay prepares underneath, so it adds no wait. Any key skips it (the key is swallowed). Plays for movies, episodes and My Media. Live channels are off by default (separate toggle) and it never plays on channel up/down or reconnects. Settings > App: "Intro before shows" (default ON) and "Intro before live channels too" (default OFF). Both are global.
- **Motorsports** (`js/motor.js`): F1, NASCAR Cup and IndyCar from ESPN racing calendars and scoreboards (live session detection, broadcasters). MotoGP, WRC, WEC and World Rallycross from TheSportsDB's free public key. Guide scan of the main sports and motorsport channels for live races. The Sports page order is now NHL -> MTB -> Motorsports (live now & coming up, **Live rally racing**, Rally coming up, Motorsport channels) -> Live now -> the rest. Events open the channel picker (series names, GP/rally name, broadcaster, date).
- **Live Events banner: more events, ranked by significance.** Numbered UFC, the Super Bowl, the Olympics (when provider Olympic channels exist) and the World Cup Final rank highest. Then provider PPV fights, the Stanley Cup Final, World Series, NBA Finals and World Cup, the Indy 500, Le Mans and the Daytona 500, the NFL/NBA/MLB/NHL playoffs, F1 Grands Prix, UFC Fight Nights, boxing (TheSportsDB), NASCAR/IndyCar/MotoGP and WRC. Top events show up to 4 days ahead, smaller ones 1.5-2 days. Up to 16 events, deduplicated.
- `tools/render_mini.py` renders the mini intro preview.

## 4.8
- **Cinematic intro** (`js/intro.js`, ~4.4 s, any key skips, Settings toggle unchanged): an anamorphic light streak ignites, then the logo slams in from depth as solid extruded 3D letters ("ta"). A second, bigger hit ("dum") brings a flash, a shockwave ring, a glow bloom, a horizontal lens flare and a light sweep across the letters. The logo slowly pushes in while light ribbons rise behind it, then the camera dives through the logo into a 3D tunnel of colored light ribbons rushing past.
- **New generated sound** (Web Audio, nothing sampled): rumble swell, then a tuned "ta" hit and a deep "dum" (sub drop, distorted body, D-major add9 chord bloom with a filter swell), rising bell shimmer, and a panning whoosh with a rising tone under the ribbon dive. `introSynth(ctx, dest, t0)` also renders offline.
- Opacity is only animated on flat wrappers, never on `preserve-3d` elements, so Chromium keeps real depth.
- `tools/render_intro.py` renders a 1080p30 preview MP4 with audio (frame-exact via the Web Animations API, plus OfflineAudioContext).

## 4.7
- **Playback resilience:** bigger AVPlay buffers (`setBufferingParam` / `setTimeoutForBuffering`) with a Normal / Large / Maximum setting; live auto-reconnect on error, stall, frozen clock or provider-side stream end, with back-off (1 s up to 15 s), a "Reconnecting…" badge and an HLS↔TS switch after 3 failures; clean stop and a 1.2 s gap before reopening (1-connection accounts); debounced channel zapping; 25 s prepare timeout; VOD resumes at the last position after an error.
- **Background fetches paused while playing** (EPG queue only for the channel on screen, NAS refresh/matching deferred).
- **Diagnostics:** last 120 playback events kept on the TV (`nf_pdiag`), 24 h summary in Settings.
- **Netflix-style scrubbing** for movies, episodes and NAS files: ◀ ▶ / REW FF move a marker while the video plays, one seek on release / OK / 1 s idle, hold to accelerate (10 s → 30 s → 1 min → 2 min → 5 min), tap = 10 s with a ripple, BACK cancels. Media keys registered (`tv.inputdevice` privilege).
- **127 avatars** (111 new, original flat art generated by `tools/mkav2.py`) in a category-row picker: Smileys, Animals, Sports, Yukon & outdoors, Food, Space, Characters & monsters, Flags, Letters. Existing avatars 0–15 unchanged.
- **Secrets moved out of the source:** provider login and NAS address now come from `js/config.js` (gitignored, see `config.example.js`); `tools/build.py` injects them and the `<access>` origins.
